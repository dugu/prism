# Models and exports

## Pretrained weights (NOT redistributed — public downloads)

All detectors use the official Ultralytics released weights, unmodified. No training
or fine-tuning was performed at any point in this study.

    for m in yolov5nu yolov5su yolov8n yolov8s yolov8m yolo11n yolo11s yolo11m yolov10n yolov10s; do
      curl -L -o $m.pt https://github.com/ultralytics/assets/releases/download/v8.3.0/$m.pt
    done

`sha256sums.txt` lists the checksum of every weight file and every export actually
used, so the downloads can be verified against what was measured.

## Exports included here

- `yolo11n_640.onnx` — ONNX opset 17, static shapes, produced by
  `YOLO('yolo11n.pt').export(format='onnx', imgsz=640, opset=17, simplify=False, dynamic=False)`
- `yolo11n_640_int8_openvino_model/` — OpenVINO IR, INT8 post-training quantisation,
  calibrated on the COCO-Traffic subset (`export(format='openvino', imgsz=640, int8=True, data=coco_traffic.yaml)`)
- `yolo11n_416_int8_openvino_model/` — same at 416 px

The FP32 OpenVINO IRs and the yolov8n exports are regenerated by
`e2_edge_backends.py` in one pass; their checksums are in `sha256sums.txt`.

Quantisation is calibration-data dependent, so an INT8 export regenerated elsewhere
will not be bit-identical; the two included here are the exact artefacts behind the
INT8 rows of Table 5.
