# Exploratory evaluation protocol

Specified before executing the extended study on 14 September 2026. Existing scenes have already informed system development; these are exploratory reuse analyses, not an untouched test set or preregistered study.

1. Reconstruct all candidate states from the 32 cached sequences. Do not change the detector, geometry, priority function, or original PRISM defaults.
2. Compare independently implemented risk-threshold, risk-hysteresis, and risk-dwell selectors with PRISM. All use the same detail mapping, quality gate, overlap limit and capacities. Hysteresis lowers the raw continuation threshold by 0.15. Dwell adds the original eight-frame preemptible preference and 0.06 ranking bonus. Only PRISM has central attenuation.
3. Grid: activation thresholds 0, .12, .18, .24, .30, .36, .42, .48, .54, .60; surrogate budgets .0165, .033, .055; cue limits 1, 3, 5. Continuation uses max(0, activation-.15). PRISM effective off threshold is continuation+.06. Include its exact default separately.
4. Leave one source scene out with all four corruption variants grouped. Select separately within each family on the other seven scenes. Maximize mean sequence coverage minus lambda times mean cues minus .005 times transitions/s, with lambda in [0, .025, .05, .10]. Coverage averages only eligible sequences; count and transitions use all sequences. These utility preferences are illustrative, not empirically calibrated driver costs. Break ties deterministically by configuration order.
5. Report pooled frame retention, macro sequence cue/transition means, and scene-cluster paired bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples, seed 20260914); no non-inferiority claim. Report default and selected results including losses.
6. Temporal metrics: longest consecutive eligible omission (reference identity may change); detected-reference bouts with a fixed recorded matched track identity, conditional first-display delay, never-displayed bouts, below-activation cue-frame fraction. These are not independent hazard events or ground-truth tracking metrics.
7. Render all default-policy frames on black canvases at original dimensions, with panel/ribbon disabled. Count nonzero painted union pixels and multiply painted pixels from individual cue masks. This is renderer support, not perceived clutter.
8. Factorial PRISM ablation of persistence, centre attenuation and quality gate; additional one-factor perturbations of dwell, bonus, central penalty and quality threshold.
9. Independent analytic geometry stress tests use fixed physical heights [1.2,1.5,1.7,2.0] metres, independently of the person estimator prior, constant/accelerated closing, bounding-height noise, dropout, truncation and identity resets. Record depth bias, finite TTC availability and error relative to instantaneous Z/closing speed. This is controlled model stress testing, not independent real-world validation.
10. Audit the real clip separately using a fixed one-second sampling schedule and visible ego-vehicle false detections; preserve ambiguity and disclose machine-assisted annotation. No hazard ground truth or detector-wide precision/recall is claimed.
